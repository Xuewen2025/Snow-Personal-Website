Xuewen Luo

Ph.D. Student · Transportation Engineering · University of Utah

I am a Ph.D. student in Transportation Engineering at the University of Utah (since Fall 2025). I am currently an ML Perception Intern at Glydways, working on in-cabin perception. Before that, I received my Master of Artificial Intelligence (High Distinction) from Monash University and my B.M. from Yunnan University.

My research focuses on autonomous driving and vision-language models, including multimodal perception and reasoning, V2X cooperation, knowledge-augmented LLMs for driving, and human-centered understanding of autonomous vehicles.
